=== 2. 51% attack on proof of work (hashrate) ===
  A 51% miner can outrun the honest chain and rewrite history.
  attacker controls 51% of computing power (PoW)
  after 100 rounds: attacker 51 blocks, honest 49 blocks (+1 victim block)
  public height 52, attacker height 53
  verdict: SUCCESS - longer fork replaces the public chain
  49% is not enough over the same number of rounds:
  attacker controls 49% of computing power (PoW)
  after 100 rounds: attacker 49 blocks, honest 51 blocks (+1 victim block)
  public height 54, attacker height 51
  verdict: FAILED - attacker fork is not longer

=== 3. 51% attack on proof of stake (amount/stake) ===
  Same idea, but the weight is the stake instead of the hashrate.
  attacker controls 51% of stake (PoS)
  after 100 rounds: attacker 51 blocks, honest 49 blocks (+1 victim block)
  public height 52, attacker height 53
  verdict: SUCCESS - longer fork replaces the public chain
  45% stake cannot take over the longest chain:
  attacker controls 45% of stake (PoS)

=== 4. Double spending with a 51%+ attacker ===
  1. Alice pays the merchant: Alice->Merchant:10 (payment)
     The payment is in public block 3 and the merchant ships.
  2. Alice (60% hashrate) secretly mines a fork from block 2
     with a conflicting spend: Alice->Mallory:10 (same coins)
  3. Attack result: her fork is longer, the network reorgs
  4. Payment still confirmed after the reorg: no
     Conflicting spend in canonical chain: yes
     The merchant lost the goods and the payment was undone.

  With less than 50% hashrate, withholding still lets the
